3

Safety rule

If the AI is not sure — or says "no tumor" without being very sure — the scan is flagged for a specialist.

4

Simple words

Gemini explains the result using only facts checked by our group. It never sees the image.

What it is good for

  • Helping busy hospitals decide which scans to read first
  • Helping patients understand their result and prepare questions
  • Explaining in English, Thai, Burmese, and Japanese

Limitations (please read)

  • Trained on a public dataset of 2D MRI slices, not real hospital data
  • The AI can be wrong — sometimes confidently wrong
  • Glioma is the hardest type for the AI
  • Not a medical device. It does not diagnose.
