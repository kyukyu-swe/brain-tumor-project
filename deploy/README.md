# Brain Tumor MRI Assistant (Group 1 student project)

- **Triage list:** sorts many brain MRI scans so likely tumors and uncertain scans are read first.
- **Understand my scan:** explains the AI result in simple words (English, Thai, Burmese, Japanese).

⚠️ Prototype for education only, **not a medical device**. Trained on the public
[Brain Tumor MRI Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/masoudnickparvar/brain-tumor-mri-dataset) (CC0).
